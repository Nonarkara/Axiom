### Thailand tax quick-reference (full version in §4 of FINANCE.md)

- **CIT (Corporate Income Tax):** 20% standard, with SME rate of 0% on first ฿300K + 15% on next ฿2M for the first 5 accounting periods (eligible if registered capital ≤ ฿5M and revenue ≤ ฿30M). Axiom qualifies today.
- **WHT (Withholding Tax):** when a client pays you ฿145,500 instead of the ฿150,000 you invoiced, that ฿4,500 is **WHT they remitted on your behalf** — book as ฿150,000 revenue + ฿4,500 WHT receivable. Reclaim on PND 53.
- **VAT:** mandatory registration at ฿1.8M annual revenue. Axiom is below the threshold. Re-evaluate at every quarter-end.
- **PDPA:** ~฿50K for a privacy review before any Thai-government contract above ฿1M. Fines up to ฿5M for non-compliance.
