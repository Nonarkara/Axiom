# Authored design, not a borrowed skin

This quality method sits above Axiom's existing project contract. It does not
replace the Thailand-flag / paper theme, seven-language content, live map,
satellite HUD, canvas, auto-tour or operational state vocabulary.

## Before changing a surface

1. Name the person, situation, outcome and likely obstacle. Identify whether this
   region operates, explains, persuades or offers an experience.
2. Inventory real content and behaviour that must survive. Prototype two distinct
   information arrangements with that same content—not two theme swaps.
3. Give geometry, space, typography and colour a communication job. A diagram must
   explain a real relationship. A warning retains its meaning and a second carrier.
4. Use the words people need for the action, result and recovery. Test direct entry:
   which system, which page, where am I, what next, how do I return?
5. Prove semantics, keyboard/focus, contrast, reflow, language and complete states.
   Separately inspect composition and observe a real person doing the core task.
   A CSS audit is not a taste certificate or a human usability test.

Bauhaus informs joined craft/implementation, truthful materials and deliberate
relationships. It is not permission to add decorative circles or flatten a rich
operational product. Wada's expressive colour relationships belong in appropriate
editorial regions, never as random replacements for identity or state colours.
