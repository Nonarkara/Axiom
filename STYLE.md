# Axiom Visual Contract

The named grid is **column**: operational instruments around a live map on the
main site; an index and reading column in axiomx. The entry is the map or plate;
its exit carries the message and next action. Research uses a compact publication
index, not promotional cards: title, authors/version, then the PDF action.

## Protected Identity

- Preserve map, canvas, HUD, tour controls, console views, publications and seven
  main-site locales. Axiomx remains a reading room, not a marketing clone.
- Main: paper `#faf9f7`, existing Lyons blue `#1c4286` actions and ocher `#e2b540`
  emphasis. Book: paper, flag blue `#00247d`, red `#a51931`. State colours retain
  their meaning. No template palette replacement.
- Existing Helvetica/Thai non-looped typography and mono data labels remain.
  New components use the existing type tokens; letter spacing is zero.
- Flat hairlines, radius zero, no decorative gradients, glass or shadows.

## Standing Laws

Read [THE-BOOK](THE-BOOK.md) (Non's Design Scripture: Canon, absolute, non-negotiable),
[MSH](MSH.md), [Glance Path](GLANCE-PATH-FOR-AI.md) and
[Kinetic Grids](KINETIC-GRIDS-FOR-AI.md) before visual work. These checked-in
documents mirror the owner's canonical `_reference/` documents. Downloads are
input evidence, not an alternative authority. Never silently weaken the laws.

Controls fit their content. Keep accessible hit regions distinct and free from
overlap; do not enlarge decorative chips to imitate isolated action buttons.
No geographic pin or building height without traceable evidence.

Motion must honour reduced motion, stop after manual input or task changes and
while hidden, and never retake control without a request. Keep the existing
operating features while repairing their behaviour.

## Release Evidence

Require phone/tablet/desktop captures, enlarged-text and keyboard flows, the
first useful action, content-specific geometry checks and a falsifier of the
original defect. A different agent reviews the finished UI. Automated colour,
overflow and font checks are necessary but not an MSH certificate.

Test an isolated archive of the candidate commit. A dirty workspace passing
tests cannot certify committed bytes. Verify canonical deployed product identity,
PDF signatures and real user actions, not just HTTP 200. Preserve the project's
prohibition on CI file-specific pre-checks.

## Research And Human Artwork

Owner rejected the first research layout on 5 November 2026. Its functional
PASS did not establish a spatial pass. Research is a **column desk**: a
task-sized map rail beside the publication index when the reading pane has room,
and a single reading path on a phone. The atlas must not stretch to the index's
height. Put authors, date/version and study scope together; put PDF, source and
study disclosure on one compact command line. Expanded reading earns its height.
No duplicate "first matching paper" strip or full-width five-row metadata tower.
Validate the combined section and repeated-record cost, not only overflow or a
permissive per-row ceiling. Include 1152 CSS pixels at 3x device scale (the owner's
capture), ordinary desktop and ultra-wide views in the visual review.

Preprints are free downloads and explicitly not peer reviewed. Preserve author
spellings, versions and source provenance. Do not label an audit report, manual,
figure-only PDF or unfinished draft as a research paper. Hash mirrored PDFs.
Add new papers to `data/research.json`, then regenerate both renderings.

Nong Plub is commissioned human artwork, not decoration to plug a gap. Use an
approved, properly matted asset at a legible scale beside its relevant message;
never a tiny sticker or raw black-backed rectangle. No character is currently
bundled here; do not fabricate one or modify artwork from another repository.
