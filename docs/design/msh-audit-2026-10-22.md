## Found, changed, verified

### Found — the phone masthead was two bands

**Element:** `.masthead__inner` at 390 × 844.
**Geometry:** the band was **80px** (9.5% of the viewport) and held two rows.
**Task cost:** the identity and the language control are one group, and they
were on two rows. 170px of brand plus 218px of language switch against a 358px
row does not fit, so the switch dropped to a second line — at the **left**, with
140px of nothing beside it, and 30px of the screen spent on the split.

**Why it happened:** a descriptor. `.brand__ver` ("Decision Systems") is 103px of
the 170px brand, and the hero states the same words 300px below it.

**Changed:** below 480px the descriptor is gone and the wordmark and the
language switch share the row they belong on. The masthead is **50px** (5.9%),
one band, at 320, 360, 390, 414 and 480px. The switch now ends exactly at the
row's right edge — there is no trailing void.

At 1024 and 1440 the hero carries `71 SYSTEMS IN PRODUCTION` on the left and
`71 LIVE SYSTEMS IN PRODUCTION` inside the console on the right, in the same
frame, saying the same thing in two voices.

**Why it is not a massacre target.** The console is a demonstration of the
product, and its figures are the product's own output; the hero's figure is the
pitch's. Removing either one would be an editorial decision about what the
catalogue is for, not a repair of invented geometry, and MSH §5.6 says MSH
authorises removing waste but does not override the anti-regression rules or the
owner's judgement about copy. **This one is Dr Non's to make.** It is recorded
here rather than silently changed.

## Method

`.qa/msh-evidence.mjs` measures the rendered interface and writes the
screenshots; the spatial gate measures the geometry it cannot see by eye. Both
are repeatable, and both are in the repository so the next agent can argue with
the numbers instead of with a memory of them.

```bash
node .qa/msh-evidence.mjs                 # both surfaces, live
node .qa/msh-evidence.mjs --local         # against the local servers
node scripts/spatial/spatial-check.mjs --base http://127.0.0.1:5312/ --routes "/,/admin/"
node .qa/spatial-sweep.mjs --base http://127.0.0.1:5311/ --routes "$(cat /tmp/rr-routes.txt)" --chunk 4
```
