# MSH compactness pass - 2026-10-27

Scope: the main Axiom site. Keep the existing theme, Leaflet map, 180px
phone map window, tour, Canvas animation, HUD, four console views, 75 system
cards and screenshots, native folds, seven locales, and all earned copy.
The separate axiomx reading room is unchanged.

## Diagnosis and decisions

The first oversized region was the console's repeated generic header band.
Its name now shares the existing reference strip and remains visibly rendered
at its original 10px size. The six domain records use two columns on desktop.
The selected scope and reference label travel together across intermediate widths.

The next repeated reading band was the three full-width process rows. Compared
two browser prototypes: three adjacent steps, and two FAQ groups. Combined
them: each step remains a readable paragraph; cost/data/security form one FAQ
group and delivery/continuity/actions form the other. On phones they retain
their original reading order. Proof figures sit beside their explanations
instead of adding a separate line above each explanation.

Field evidence now has one shared title, with video/credits/actions together
beside the supporting points above 900px. The lead-in immediately precedes the
points (10px separation). On phones, paragraphs span the full reading width
instead of reserving an empty hanging-number gutter. Reduced section-heading,
contact, footer and evidence-strip padding; no type-size reduction or new fold.

The theatre buttons now have an explicit 1.4 line height. Thai fallback fonts
previously made a fresh load 8px taller than a resized page at 480px. The
unmodified spatial cross-check caught it; fixing the actual button geometry
made fresh/resized measurements agree. Native CV summaries, video play and
tour controls retain or gain at least 44px targets.

  All console views work; expanded folds do not overflow; affected native
  controls are at least 44px. The Canvas is nonblank and moving. Video remains
  a poster until play is requested, then creates an autoplay iframe.
- Original-layout falsifier: all four cases fail measured height limits.
- Spatial falsifier: seven page defects, eleven broken-baseline cases and one
  instrument case caught under Chromium's forced reduced-motion preference.
  The normal-motion falsifier twice stopped at its overflow fixture because
  the injected 4000px element and animated Leaflet proxy disagreed between
  resized/fresh loads; those runs do not count as passes. Only the falsifier
  launch used a `CHROME_PATH` wrapper adding `--force-prefers-reduced-motion`.
  The real-page 441-measurement sweep and moving-Canvas checks used normal
  motion. The instrument, fixtures, expectations and production app were
  not changed to accommodate that fixture.

- Catalogue thumbnails: 8 passed. Language gate: passed all seven locales.
- Independent review: all four initial findings closed (cache keys, visible
  console name, field lead-in adjacency, native CV target sizes). Subsequent
  review checked EN/TH/VI at 758/820/882/900/901/902px and Thai fresh/resized

## Limits

Browser checks use headless Chromium, not a real phone or a human usability
session. The local Xcode installation does not provide `simctl`, so iOS
Simulator validation is unavailable. No fluent-language editorial review is
claimed. This is a scoped compactness improvement, not a claim that every
possible MSH issue on either site is resolved.
