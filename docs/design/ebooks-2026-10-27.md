# axiomx e-books

## Scope and composition

The reader wants to find and open Dr Non's three e-books from the systems index.
Mean, Ninja Innovation, and Slowdown live in one E-books group, after the shared
systems. Each uses the existing title, synopsis, reading action, artwork, credit,
and adjacent-entry navigation. No CSS, public-site files, shared catalogue data,
translations, maps, or earned interactions changed.

An axiomx-only extension was chosen over adding shared catalogue records: the
owner explicitly requested this surface only. `ebooks.js` derives its numbers
from the shared catalogue; parity tests remain intact. New scope tests prohibit
these three cards on the main site. The reading room now has 78 catalogue entries
(75 shared plus three e-books); these are derived, not typed into its metadata.

## Sources

Titles and synopses were checked against the three live books on 27 October:
[Mean](https://mean.nonarkara.org/), [Ninja](https://ninja.nonarkara.org/), and
[Slowdown](https://slowdown.nonarkara.org/). No login or external embed is needed.

The independent artwork researcher resolved exact Commons files with
`.qa/commons-verify.mjs` and checked their MediaInfo/Wikidata collection chains:

- Mean: [The Rosetta Stone](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Rosetta_Stone.jpg),
  public domain. Object 196 BCE; Donald Macbeth photograph published 1922.
  MediaInfo M119273729, object Q48584, British Museum Q6373. The comparison of
  languages and scripts is the mechanism, not a claim that the stone is a dictionary.
- Ninja: [Goya, El Afilador](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:El_Afilador,_Francisco_de_Goya.jpg),
  public domain, 1808-1812. M152015, Q4402545, Museum of Fine Arts Budapest Q840886.
  A modest tool provides a concrete service; the binding is ideological.
- Slowdown: [Chardin, The House of Cards](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jean_Sim%C3%A9on_Chardin,_The_House_of_Cards,_probably_1737,_NGA_97.jpg),
  CC0, probably 1737. M81325757, Q12859917, National Gallery of Art Q214867,
  accession 1937.1.90. Careful observation and placement are the depicted activity.

All three images are distinct from existing plates. Downloads were resized to
1600px high and encoded as WebP, with actual output dimensions in `plates.js`.
The art and script cache versions were bumped. No new third-party runtime origin
or dependency was added; the existing CSP remains unchanged.

## Verification

- `npm test`: 56 passed. The new plate guard first failed with the missing Mean
  plate, then passed after all three credited artworks and assets were present.
- `npm run check`: 28 files syntax checked; the new browser QA script was also
  checked directly.
- `npm run qa:plates`: 10 passed, zero failed.
- `npm run qa:journeys`: 191 passed, zero failed locally; 124 entries rendered.
- `npm run qa:ebooks`: all three books at 390x844, 1024x900, and 1440x900;
  deep links, Open destinations, loaded art, fitted labels, search, Escape,
  arrow navigation, history, phone return, 44px index targets, and dark mode.
- The unchanged spatial instrument measured 63 layouts across all 21 widths,
  with 15 fresh-load cross-checks. No findings. Only the three new routes were
  added to the baseline; old measurements and reasons are byte-equivalent as data.
  This was a scoped sweep, not a full-project stale-reason audit.

The e-book evidence script records screenshots and geometry in
`/tmp/axiomx-ebooks-evidence/`. Chrome costs 90px: 10.7% of the phone height and
10% at the other two sizes. All titles and reading actions are visible together;
action bottoms range from 295px to 370px. Images earn the remaining vertical
reading space, with credits below rather than overlapping the work. The hierarchy
is title, synopsis/action, artwork; captions fit their image widths. No overflow
or clipped title was found. Existing spacing and type were conserved, not restyled.

Independent code review found no confirmed bugs or scope violations. Its first
screenshot review requested a fresh Ninja capture; the regenerated phone image
shows the reading action above the artwork at 320px. Canonical live verification
is recorded in the release close-out. Real-person usability review was not performed.
